In this chapter, the focus shifts towards robotics. Here a device is constructed in which the majority of the outer surface consists of the same polyimide in which chemical modification was previously attempted. This section aims to make use of the highly resilient properties that are already present within the polyimide material and demonstrate the capabilities of a device constructed with them. 

This chapter covers the design and construction of a continuum robot with a protective outer layer constructed from folded polyimide sheets. This protective layer is tested for temperature stability and the manoeuvrability of this device is also tested in 3D space. The device is then demonstrated in an arbitrary setting and a medical setting, a space that can require resilient materials.
