\subsection{Temperature Stability of the Bellows}

\begin{figure}

To evaluate the stability of the acutator, tis was placed in the oven. After removing and running the sample 3 times after an hour each time. We determined that the actuator was very temperatrue stable.

Data from the load cell was processed in python and plotted in graphs. The plots were trimmed to start once the force was over 0.2N. This gave plots as shown in \ref{fig:heat_test}. 

% 1)State the key finding in one sentence
This report found that there was minimal change to the performance of the kapton sleeve after heat treatment, it was observed that the variability on results is less than x when calculated using python
% 2)Offer a mechanistic explanation
This is due to kapton having a thermal decomposition at x temperature, hence the lack of behavioural change after heating to 200$\degree$C is unsurprising.
% 3)Compare to literature or expectation
Kapton based structures have been shown to resist many thermal cycles before degrading \cite{butler2016highly}
% 4)Acknowledge limitations or anomalies
Limitations of this experiment are similar to the stiffness characterisation tests due to the use of the same experimental set-up

% 5)State the implication for your device/application
The lack of behavioural change implies that the assembly would be well suited to a thermally harsh environment. Paired with other properties such as the resistance to UV degradation this motivates the use of polyimide as a material for space robotics.
