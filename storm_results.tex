\subsection{Workspace Characterisation}

Workspace characterisation of a device is important to gain an understanding of the total regions that a device can get to


The workspace of a device is the set of all reachable tip positions that the device can achieve \cite{granna2014characterizing}. 

We tested 4 different assemblies which gradually increase the stiffness to demonstrate the stabilisation of the polyimide sleeve to aid the controllability of the silicon actuation device. 


\subsection{Navigation}

To demonstrate the robots ability to navigate an arbitrary path, a route was set up using 3D printed obstacles to simulate a path that a real world use case could need. The goal of the path was to show that fluid could be delivered through a channel in the silicon inner tube.

This path started shorted than the relaxed length of the actuator and so required the operator to pull on the silicon tube to retract the device before steering the device using the tendons. 
 
Extending the device further than the relaxed length was difficult due to the soft the silicon tube buckling under compression. This meant that the maximum path length was limited to around 120mm. %check this


Practically the effects of the stiffness of the bellows are felt, when we use our device for naviagation we found that retracting the silicon and extending is by pushing it was easy until the relaxed point. After this, overcoming the force of the sleeve pushing back on the silicon actuation device causes buckling internally due to the lack of rigidity of the silicon tube.
