Our methodology is inspired by similar "bellows-style" approaches to construct continuum devices (references) with the  key difference in the use of Polyimide sheets (Kapton) forming the main body of the device.

The construction method is as follows: a sheet of polyimide is marked with the desired folding pattern (as shown in figure), the sheet is folded by hand before being shaped into a tube and glued along an overlapping edge. The folds are encouraged into place and one end is sealed. This is the assembled with 3D printed parts to form the actuator.

This work focuses on the use of Kapton (thickness = ) due to its good robustness and high temperature capabilities.
